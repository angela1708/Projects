Forage - Introduction to Software Engineering: Tips for Financial Cybersecurity

By: Angel Avila. Student at Indiana University Indiana (IUI) majoring in Computer Science Bachelors of Arts


Have you ever wondered how an account(s) of yours was hacked? Did it by chance contain personal information? You have the latest security features on your account(s) to prevent this from happening...yet it still happened. This is what's called Customer Account Takeover (CAS). CAS occurs when you receive some phishing text or email that looks very convincing, e.g. your bank institution, SNAP email, your local McDonalds, etc. There are many ways you can protect yourself from letting these scams convince you to give them your information.

1. Look Carefully

Always check if the link or message is real. Do not go straight ahead and click on it or respond. Delete the message and report it instead.


2. Make Unique Passwords

Every single account of yours should be different and not the same.


3. Use a Multifactor Authentication (MFA)

Just a password could prevent hackers from getting in, but installing an MFA protects your account even more.


4. Update your Device

Every OS contains helpful security to prevent these measures from happening.


5. Be Careful With Other Devices

If you happen to use public devices, NEVER use them to see your accounts as those devices could contain software that takes your login information and hacks you from there.


6. Browse Carefully

Make sure to search your personal information in a secure browser(s).


7. Use Public Wi-Fi with Caution

Need internet at the mall? Use it for simple searches and never personal logins as public wi-fi lowers security.


8. Review Account Activities

See if there is anything different in the activity. If you notice something, someone has entered your account.


9. Be Cautious When Handing-out Information

Just because it says "Enter SSN: " doesn't mean you should right away. Make sure it's safe and secure.


10. Research Website

You find something convincing, right? Never hurts to check and research whether or not the website is legit.


11. Give Information To Encrypted Websites Only.

If you need to enter your information on a website, look for the "s" at the beginning of the website as it means secure.


This list is a few ways you can protect yourself from letting these hackers steal your information. Happy protecting your information journey!
